// The Oracle's Gift

I've seen compliance
from both sides.

I got into cybersecurity because I'm naturally curious and stubborn about getting things right. I didn't plan to end up securing power grids but somewhere between a compliance audit and a lot of NIST frameworks, I found my niche.

I've lived compliance from both sides of the table: building security programs from within organizations, and auditing critical infrastructure from the outside. That dual perspective is rare, and it's what I bring to every engagement.

My specialty sits at the intersection that few GRC analysts occupy understanding how NIST 800-53 controls apply to a Virtual Power Plant, how CMMC requirements cascade through a DoD supply chain, and how a regulator like Maryland PSC actually uses the outputs of a risk assessment.
